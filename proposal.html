MXT × Email Donut

Email & SMS Revamp

Proposal for Jack Davies · Two-month project · July 2026

This is the build behind the flow plan we walked through. Two months, fixed scope, fixed price. It covers three things in order: recovering the sending reputation, turning SMS on, and rebuilding the flows so the quiz data you already collect actually reaches the person who generated it.

The engine writes roughly 40 finished copy blocks per person. The live flows use 7, several of them wired wrong, and 79 broken placeholders are rendering as visible text in real sends. Source: message-by-message census of all 46 live emails and 40 SMS, July 15.
01

What the two months buy

  1. Deliverability recoveryOpens sit near 8%. Authentication is clean, so this is reputation, not setup, and it traces back to the bounce spike from quiz testing. The fix is plain-text campaigns to the most engaged segment, twice a week, until opens recover. Expected window: 2 to 4 weeks.
  2. SMS turned onNumbers are being collected and not a single SMS has ever sent. We diagnose the break (sending number and 10DLC registration, consent gating, or message status), fix the broken tags, and switch the channel on. This is the one channel that works while email reputation is still climbing back.
  3. The missing flows builtAbandoned Checkout and Abandoned Browse. Your two highest-intent audiences currently receive nothing at all.
  4. The existing flows rebuiltQuiz Complete and Quiz Started, wired to the real copy blocks, the real properties, and the real offer. The live site sells the free 21-day trial at $6.95 shipping. Every email and SMS in the account still sells a $20 trial that no longer exists.
02

Flows are built twice, and that is the point

Written first, designed second. One price covers both builds.

Fixing campaign deliverability does not automatically carry over to flows, so we do not gamble the rebuild on it. Every flow goes live as plain text first, which is both the fastest thing to ship and the safest thing to send while reputation is still recovering. Once opens confirm the recovery, the same copy gets rebuilt as full branded design.

Phase 1 · from day one Written

Every flow message goes live as lightweight plain text. Copy, logic, branching, property wiring, and SMS are all final in this phase. This is the version that carries revenue while the domain climbs back.

Live in under 7 days from kickoff.

Phase 2 · on the deliverability signal Designed

Once open rates confirm recovery, each message is rebuilt as fully designed, on-brand HTML. The copy does not change. The design comes in on top of a version already proven to send and open.

Triggered by the data, not the calendar.

What this means commercially: every flow message in this proposal is built twice, once written and once designed. It is priced once. The per-message rate below covers both builds.

What happens after

At the end of month two we will know two things we cannot know today: whether the reputation recovered, and how much campaign volume your list size actually supports. Both answers change what a sensible ongoing arrangement looks like, which is why this is scoped as a project and not a retainer.

If recovery lands where we expect, the natural next step is a monthly cadence built around campaign volume, and the work extends past Klaviyo into Recharge and how the subscription flow feeds back into the program.
